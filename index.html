01 — The House

A house that knows how to be quiet — glass to the weather, timber underfoot, and nothing between you and the horizon but ninety metres of clean air.

The Cliffs sits alone at the end of an unsealed lane on the Great Ocean Road, turned to face the open ocean. The plan is simple and generous: a long living room that follows the sun, three bedrooms that each hold a piece of the view, and a kitchen made for slow dinners after a day on the coast.

There is no television worth mentioning and the wifi is quietly fast. Most guests find they stop checking it by the second morning.

On a clear evening you can watch the weather arrive from Antarctica.

03 — Signature Experiences

What a day on the coast asks of you: very little.

i

Sea-cliff walks

Step from the deck onto the coastal track. Headlands, hidden coves, and a lighthouse an hour along.

ii

Whale season

June through October, mothers and calves rest in the bay below. Some mornings you need not leave the window.

iii

Cellar doors

A dozen small, cool-climate wineries within half an hour — pinot, chardonnay, and long lunches with a view.

iv

Dark skies

No town glow for miles. On clear nights the Milky Way lands squarely over the water.

05 — The Descent

The Cliffs sits directly on the Great Ocean Road, where it runs hard along Victoria's southern coastline — one of the great coastal drives on earth, and your front doorstep. Remote enough to feel it, close enough to be easy. The last of the lane is unsealed; a small car manages it fine, and full directions arrive with your booking.
